# Chapter 8 materials: Cedarline Property Management

Everything you need to follow Project 7, extracting and consolidating contract and invoice data.

- `01-sample-documents/` holds two monthly batches of fictional PDFs. Use `month-1-june` for the whole chapter and `month-2-july` to test your skill at the end.
- `02-prompts/` has every prompt from the chapter, in the order you send them.
- `03-skill/` holds a reference `document-extractor` skill to compare with the one Claude writes for you.
- `04-samples/` shows the workbook at three points: after the exception rules, after review, and after the July run.
- `05-answer-key/` lists the value Claude should extract for every field, the exceptions each batch should raise, and the verification numbers.

Cedarline, its vendors, and every amount in these documents are invented. Nothing here describes a real company.
