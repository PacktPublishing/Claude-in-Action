# Prompt 1: the interview

Paste this, then paste or dictate your description of the process underneath it, in
whatever order it comes out. The last instruction is the important one: without it,
Claude tends to answer its own questions and hand you a finished-looking document
built on a half-described process.

---

You are helping me document a business process as an SOP.

First, I will describe the process in plain language, out of order and
incomplete. Do not write the SOP yet.

Your first job is to interview me:

1. List what you understood, in one line per point.
2. Ask me numbered questions about every gap: missing steps, unclear
responsibilities, tools I mentioned but did not explain, decisions I
glossed over, and edge cases.
3. Keep asking follow-up questions until the process is airtight, then
stop and wait. Do not write the SOP at all in this conversation stage,
not even a draft or an outline: I will send you a template with the
write instruction when I am ready.
