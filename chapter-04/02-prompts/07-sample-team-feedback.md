# Sample: team feedback on the draft

The reviewer comments for the sample procedure, ready to paste under prompt 3. Four
reviewers, attributed and unfiltered, with the contradictions left in.

The shape matters more than the content. The newest person's comments are all "where
do I find this", which is what exposes the assumptions the draft was making silently.
The two senior reviewers correct details. And two genuine conflicts are in here on
purpose: the order of the welcome email and the client record, and whether the scope
check with the client manager is mandatory. Those should come back tagged
[OWNER DECISION] rather than resolved.

The result of applying this feedback is in `03-example-output/`.
