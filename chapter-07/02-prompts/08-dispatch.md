# Prompt 8: building the deck from your phone

Dispatch connects the Claude mobile app to the Cowork session on your desktop. Check the Dispatch article at support.claude.com for current setup steps and plan availability before you rely on it.

Requirements: both devices on the latest Claude app, the desktop app open, and the one-time pairing done.

From your phone, once the roadmap is already approved at your desk:

```
In project silverpine-roadmap, build the presentation from the current
roadmap.
```

The deck lands in the project folder, and Dispatch shows it in the chat on your phone.

Dispatch is for this mechanical step only. Do the approval at your desk.
