# Answer key

- `expected-results.md` the correct count, respondent count, strongest emotion and rows for every theme, for the Q2 sheet and after the July batch
- `theme-key-Q2.csv` and `theme-key-July.csv` every row of the sheet with the themes it mentions, the emotion rating for each, and a respondent identifier. Open one next to the sheet when you audit
- `audit-checklist.md` the ten-minute audit from the chapter, as a list
- `adapting-to-your-data.md` what to change when you point the workflow at your own feedback

## How to read the theme keys

`themes` and `emotions` are semicolon-separated and line up: a row tagged `deposits;sync` with `5;3` mentions deposits at emotion 5 and sync at emotion 3. Names starting `w_` are weak signals. `respondent` is the email where one was given, otherwise `anon-row` plus the row number, since an anonymous response can only be counted as its own respondent.

Emotion follows the prompt's scale: 5 means the customer reports lost revenue or threatens to leave.
