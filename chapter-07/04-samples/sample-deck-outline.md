# Sample deck outline: silverpine-roadmap-Q2.pptx

Seven slides: four fixed plus one per P1 item.

## Slide 1: Title

- Silverpine product roadmap, Q2 2026
- 189 customer responses, April 1 to June 26, 2026

## Slide 2: Method

- Source: the customer feedback form in the monthly newsletter
- Every theme counted, quoted, and traced to sheet rows
- Ranked twice: by mention count, and by count weighted by the strongest emotion
- Main items need at least three distinct respondents

## Slide 3: P1, Google Calendar sync failures

- Signal: 19 mentions from 18 respondents, strongest emotion 5/5
- "A wedding booking vanished from my Google Calendar and I almost missed it"
- "If Google Calendar sync breaks one more time I am moving to another tool"

Speaker notes: 19 mentions, rows 12, 13, 15, 17, 30, 45, 47, 60, 87, 92, 107, 112, 127, 130, 133, 137, 148, 163, 177.
Drops below deposits if the sync fix turns out to be more than a week of work.

## Slide 4: P1, Payments and deposits at booking

- Signal: 30 mentions from 24 respondents, strongest emotion 5/5
