## P1

### P1: Google Calendar sync failures
Product area: Calendar and scheduling
Signal: 19 mentions from 18 respondents | Strongest emotion: 5/5 (row 12)
Type: Complaint
Placement decided by: approval override; weighted order placed it second

Bookings disappear from or fail to reach Google Calendar, causing double bookings and one lost wedding job. Two customers say they will leave over it.

Evidence (rows 12, 13, 15, 17, 30, 45, 47...):
- "A wedding booking vanished from my Google Calendar and I almost missed it"
- "If Google Calendar sync breaks one more time I am moving to another tool"
- "Seriously considering cancelling because of calendar sync"

Note: deposits remains the largest single request.

### P1: Payments and deposits at booking
Product area: Booking flow
Signal: 30 mentions from 24 respondents | Strongest emotion: 5/5 (row 33)
Type: Feature request
Placement decided by: count and weighted agree

Customers lose revenue to no-shows and at least one is evaluating competitors over it. Mentions span photography, salons, tutoring, and barbershops, from first-month customers to two-year accounts, so the need is not specific to one segment or one stage.

Evidence (rows 6, 18, 33, 58, 64, 71, 77...):
- "No-shows cost me a full afternoon of studio time"
- "Saturdays are 40 percent of my revenue"
- "I am actively looking at competitors"

### P1: Booking on phones is unreliable, and there is no owner app
Product area: Mobile
Signal: 27 mentions from 27 respondents | Strongest emotion: 3/5 (row 8)
Type: Both
Placement decided by: count order

Clients struggle to book on phones and owners want an app for their own schedule. Widespread irritation, but nobody reports losing money or leaving.

Evidence (rows 8, 9, 29, 35, 41, 56, 57...):
- "Mobile page freezes on older phones"
- "The mobile page freezes after choosing a service"
- "Phone booking page jumps back to the top after every tap"

## P2

### P2: SMS reminders stop when the monthly cap is hit
Product area: Messaging
Signal: 16 mentions from 16 respondents | Strongest emotion: 4/5 (row 27)
Type: Both
Placement decided by: both orders agree

Reminders stop mid-month when the plan cap is reached. A plan-limits decision, not a bug.

Evidence (rows 24, 27, 32, 69, 74, 80, 81...):
- "The SMS cap is the most annoying limit in the plan"
- "Reminders stop sending mid-month because of the SMS limit"
- "Reminders silently stopped after I hit the monthly SMS cap"

### P2: Staff roles and permissions
Product area: Accounts and permissions
Signal: 10 mentions from 10 respondents | Strongest emotion: 3/5 (row 65)
Type: Feature request
Placement decided by: both orders agree

Every staff login has full admin rights, so assistants can see reports and change prices.

Evidence (rows 7, 36, 65, 73, 84, 94, 96...):
- "Every staff login has full admin rights"
- "A junior stylist changed my prices by accident"
- "Trainers should not see each other's client notes"

## P3

### P3: Booking page branding
Product area: Booking page
Signal: 8 mentions from 8 respondents | Strongest emotion: 2/5 (row 21)
Type: Feature request
Placement decided by: both orders agree

Owners want their own logo, colours and domain on the booking page.

Evidence (rows 21, 63, 69, 85, 91, 156, 168...):
- "Custom domain for the booking page"
- "Booking page should use my logo and colours"
- "Booking page looks the same as everyone else's"

### P3: Broken Romanian and Hungarian characters in SMS
Product area: Messaging
Signal: 6 mentions from 6 respondents | Strongest emotion: 2/5 (row 10)
Type: Complaint
Placement decided by: both orders agree

SMS reminders break Romanian and Hungarian characters, which some clients read as spam. An encoding bug, separate from the SMS cap.

Evidence (rows 10, 23, 62, 125, 146, 175):
- "Diacritics break in SMS reminders"
- "Texts to Hungarian clients mangle ő and ű"
- "Hungarian names come out broken in reminder texts"

### P3: Per-service buffer times
Product area: Calendar and scheduling
Signal: 5 mentions from 5 respondents | Strongest emotion: 2/5 (row 42)
Type: Feature request
Placement decided by: both orders agree

One global buffer between appointments does not fit services of different lengths.

Evidence (rows 42, 46, 123, 166, 188):
- "One global buffer wastes my short appointments"
- "Massages need 15 minutes between, consultations need none"
- "Buffer time after colouring should differ from a quick trim"

### P3: Single-occurrence edits on recurring bookings
Product area: Calendar and scheduling
Signal: 4 mentions from 4 respondents | Strongest emotion: 2/5 (row 89)
Type: Complaint
Placement decided by: both orders agree

Editing one occurrence of a recurring booking changes the whole series.

Evidence (rows 89, 128, 155, 158):
- "Editing one weekly session changes all of them"
- "Let me move a single occurrence of a recurring booking"
- "Changing one date in a repeating series moves the whole series"
