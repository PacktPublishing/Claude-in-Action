## Note to the interview guide generator

**The three priorities for this hire, in order:**

1. Paid campaign management on Google Ads and Meta Ads.
2. Marketing analytics: measuring campaigns in GA4 and changing decisions because of what the data says.
3. Email lifecycle: building and improving automated flows that turn signups into paying customers.

Everything else in this description is context. Questions come from these three.

---

**Role title:** Marketing Specialist
**Team and manager:** Marketing, reporting to the Head of Growth
**Location and hours:** Remote within the EU, overlapping CET working hours

**About Brightpath Analytics.** We build a marketing analytics dashboard for small and mid-size e-commerce stores, so a store owner can open one screen and know what to do that day to grow.

**What you will own.** Paid acquisition, marketing analytics, and lifecycle email. You run the paid budget, you measure what it returns, and you build the automated emails that turn signups into customers.

**In a typical month you will:**

- Own the paid search and paid social budget, roughly 8,000 euros a month across Google and Meta. You decide the split and you answer for the result.
- Report on cost per lead weekly, in GA4, and shift spend toward what is working.
- Build and improve the automated email flows that move new signups toward a paid plan. There are three flows today and nobody currently owns them.
- Write the ad copy and the email copy yourself. Design comes from a freelancer, and you brief them.
- Share weekly numbers with the team in plain language a non-technical colleague can act on.

**Must-have (we cannot consider candidates without these):**

- At least 2 years hands-on managing paid acquisition budgets on Google Ads and/or Meta Ads, with direct responsibility for the spend and the result.
- Hands-on experience using GA4 or an equivalent web analytics tool to measure campaigns and change decisions because of the data.
- Eligible to work in the EU and able to overlap with CET hours.

**Nice-to-have (a plus, not required):**

- Experience owning email lifecycle flows in a tool such as Klaviyo or HubSpot.
- An e-commerce or B2B SaaS background.
- Comfort reading dashboards and a basic grasp of attribution.

**How we hire.** A knockout screen, then a structured panel interview where every candidate answers the same role-specific questions scored on a shared rubric. We decide from evidence and send a decision within five working days of the final interview.

**What we offer.** A competitive salary band for the level, fully remote work within the EU, and ownership of a result from day one.
