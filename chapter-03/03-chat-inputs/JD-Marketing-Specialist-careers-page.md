**Marketing Specialist**

Remote within the EU

**About us**

Brightpath Analytics helps small and mid-size e-commerce stores understand their marketing. We are a growing team of around 60 people and we care about doing good work in plain language.

**The role**

We are looking for a Marketing Specialist to join our marketing team and help take Brightpath to the next stage of growth. You will work closely with colleagues across the business on a variety of campaigns and channels.

**What you will do**

- Support the marketing team in executing multi-channel campaigns and contribute to the growth of our brand presence.
- Help drive customer acquisition through a mix of paid and organic channels.
- Work with data to understand performance and share insights with the team.
- Contribute to our email marketing efforts and help nurture our audience.
- Create engaging copy that reflects our brand voice.

**What we are looking for**

- Experience in a digital marketing role.
- A data-driven mindset and strong analytical skills.
- Excellent written communication.
- A team player who thrives in a fast-moving environment.
- Familiarity with marketing tools and platforms.

**What we offer**

A competitive salary, fully remote work within the EU, and the chance to make a real impact.
