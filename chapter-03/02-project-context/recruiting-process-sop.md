# Recruiting process SOP

Brightpath Analytics is the fictional company used throughout Chapter 3. Add this file to the Hiring project context.

This is the standard path from an open role to an offer.

1. **Intake.** The hiring manager fills in the role success profile and confirms the three priorities and the knockout criteria.
2. **Job description.** Build the job description from the template, mark must-have and nice-to-have, and publish. Mark the three priorities at the top of the internal copy, which is the version the generator reads.
3. **Sourcing and applications.** Collect CVs. Acknowledge every applicant within three working days.
4. **Knockout screen.** Run each CV through the Interview Guide Generator. Any candidate who fails a knockout is declined with a short, respectful note.
5. **Guide generation.** For candidates who pass, generate the role-specific guide and scorecard.
6. **Panel interview.** Each interviewer owns a section, scores live on the 1 to 4 rubric, and writes one line of evidence per question.
7. **Debrief.** Within 24 hours, the panel assembles the sections into one scorecard and reaches a hire or no-hire call from the scores and the evidence lines.
8. **Decision and offer.** The hiring manager makes the final call, records the reason next to the scorecard, and moves to offer or decline.

**Service levels.** Acknowledge applicants within 3 working days. Send a decision within 5 working days of the final interview. Keep candidate data only as long as policy allows.
