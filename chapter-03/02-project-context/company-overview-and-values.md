# Company overview, mission, vision, and values

Brightpath Analytics is the fictional company used throughout Chapter 3. Add this file to the Hiring project context.

**Company overview.** Brightpath Analytics builds a marketing analytics dashboard for small and mid-size e-commerce stores. About 60 people, headquartered in the EU, working across CET hours. Customers are store owners and small marketing teams who want to see where their revenue comes from without hiring a data analyst.

**Mission.** Give small e-commerce teams the kind of marketing insight that used to be reserved for large companies with big budgets.

**Vision.** A store owner should be able to open one screen in the morning and know exactly what to do that day to grow.

**Values.**

- Evidence over opinion. We make decisions from numbers we can show each other.
- Ship and learn. We release small, watch what happens, and adjust.
- Plain language. We explain things so a non-technical customer understands them.
- Own the outcome. Each person owns a result they can point to, beyond a checklist of tasks.
- Respect the customer's time. Fast replies, clear answers, no runaround.
