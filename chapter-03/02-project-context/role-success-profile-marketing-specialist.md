# Role success profile: Marketing Specialist

Brightpath Analytics is the fictional company used throughout Chapter 3. Add this file to the Hiring project context.

**Purpose of the role.** Own paid acquisition and lifecycle email for Brightpath, and prove with numbers what is working.

**The three priorities.** Every interview question maps to one of these.

1. Paid campaign management on Google Ads and Meta Ads.
2. Marketing analytics: measuring campaigns in GA4 or an equivalent and acting on what the data says.
3. Email lifecycle: building and improving automated email flows that turn signups into paying customers.

**Knockout criteria.** A candidate who misses any of these is auto-declined unless the hiring manager overrides in writing.

- At least 2 years hands-on managing paid acquisition budgets on Google Ads and/or Meta Ads, with direct responsibility for the spend and the result.
- Hands-on experience using a web analytics tool such as GA4 to measure campaign performance and change decisions because of it.
- Eligible to work in the EU and able to overlap with CET working hours.

**Strongly desired, scored but not a knockout.**

- Ownership of email lifecycle flows in a tool such as Klaviyo or HubSpot.
- E-commerce or B2B SaaS background.
- Comfort reading dashboards and a basic grasp of attribution.

**What success looks like.** By 90 days, the candidate runs the paid budget without daily oversight and reports results in GA4. By one year, they have improved cost per lead and own at least two lifecycle email flows that lift conversion.

**Scorecard areas.** Paid campaign skill, analytics skill, lifecycle email skill, ownership and communication.
