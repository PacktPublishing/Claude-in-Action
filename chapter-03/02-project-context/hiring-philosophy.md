# Hiring philosophy

Brightpath Analytics is the fictional company used throughout Chapter 3. Add this file to the Hiring project context.

We hire from evidence, not from a good feeling in the room. Three principles guide every hire.

- **Structured beats spontaneous.** Every candidate for a role answers the same core questions, scored against the same rubric, so we can compare people fairly and explain our decision later.
- **Role-specific beats generic.** Questions come from the actual responsibilities of the role. What we learn from a candidate's CV shapes the follow-up probes, not the questions themselves, because questions that change per candidate cannot be compared.
- **Knockouts are checked first.** A few requirements are non-negotiable for a role. We confirm those before we invest interview time, so we are fair to candidates and to ourselves.

We run most interviews as a panel of two or three, each owning a section of the guide, and we debrief from scores rather than impressions. The hiring decision always belongs to a person, never to the tool.
