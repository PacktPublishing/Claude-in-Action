# Sample competitor cards

Four cards, one structure. The first is the one shown in the chapter.

---

**Yoga with Petra.** 13.7M subscribers, +0.74% over 90 days. Two uploads per month, all long. No Shorts since July 1. Average 330,000 views per video, which is 2.41% of subscribers. Biggest recent outliers: a posture video for chest, shoulders and upper back at 528,000 views, 1.60 times channel average; a morning grounding practice at 481,000, 1.46 times; a 20-minute anxiety session at 392,000, 1.19 times. Topic direction: posture and upper-back mobility, which has recurred all year, short morning grounding practices, and feet, where a new video did 204,000 views in ten days. Against Wildgrass: 9.4 times the audience and 2.9 times our views per video, but a third of our upload rate and a sixth of our growth. Note that the subscriber count is rounded by the provider, so the growth figure is approximate.

---

**Fern Flows.** 3.33M subscribers, +2.15% over 90 days. About 3.3 uploads per month, all long. No Shorts since April, and three weeks skipped in August and September with subscribers still rising. Average 167,000 views per video, which is 5.03% of subscribers. Biggest recent outliers: a 15-minute no-props full-body stretch at 364,000 views, 2.17 times channel average; a 30-minute morning flow at 253,000, 1.51 times; the 20-minute morning flow that ended the break at 250,000, 1.49 times. Topic direction: morning flows and no-props flexibility at 15 to 20 minutes. Vlogs, Pilates and 30-minute deep sessions underperform at 65,000 to 156,000. Against Wildgrass: 2.28 times the audience and 1.48 times our views per video, at half our upload rate and half our growth.

---

**Moon & Mat Yoga.** 3.29M subscribers, +0.30% over 90 days. About 3.7 uploads per month, 91% long and 9% Shorts. The one Short is a free-challenge promo at 47,000 views with 1,700 comments. Average 95,000 views per video, which is 2.90% of subscribers. Biggest recent outliers: a 20-minute feel-good mobility flow at 163,000 views, 1.71 times channel average; a 30-minute full-body stress and tension release at 156,000, 1.64 times; a 30-minute full-body Pilates at 135,000, 1.42 times. Topic direction: feel-good mobility flows and 30-minute stress release. The newest, a 20-minute detox flow, did 65,000 views in four days. Morning, flexibility and yin videos are weaker at 46,000 to 77,000. Against Wildgrass: 2.25 times the audience but 0.84 times our views per video, and a fourteenth of our growth.

---

**Wildgrass Yoga (us).** 1.46M subscribers, +4.29% over 90 days, the fastest growth on the board. Six uploads per month: 7 long videos and 11 Shorts over the quarter, a 39 / 61 split. Average 113,000 views per video, which is 7.77% of subscribers, the highest ratio on the board. Long videos average 191,000 views, 13.1% of subscribers. Shorts average 64,000, 4.4%. Best long videos are 15 to 25 minutes, framed around a body area or a daily routine: hips and spine, daily flow, gentle reset. Newest technique Shorts sit at 17,000 to 113,000 views after one to four days.

---

Read the fourth card against the second and third rather than the first. Fern Flows and Moon & Mat are within 40,000 subscribers of each other and grow at very different rates, which is the comparison worth understanding.
