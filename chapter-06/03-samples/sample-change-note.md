# Sample change note

What sits at the top of the board after a refresh, four weeks in.

---

**Changed since last refresh (2026-02-16)**

- Fern Flows, uploads per month 3.3 to 1.0, down about 70 percent
- Fern Flows, subscribers 3.33M to 3.40M, up 2.1 percent in the same period
- Nothing else moved more than 10 percent

---

That is the whole scan most weeks: two minutes, one line to follow up.

Opening Fern Flows's card shows the drop is a three-week gap with no uploads, and that subscribers kept rising through it. The useful response is not to match anyone's cadence. It is a second piece of evidence for what the combined view already suggested: in this niche, upload frequency is not what moves subscribers.

A refresh with an empty change note is a finished review. A month of empty change notes means either a slow niche, which is worth knowing, or signals too broad to catch what is happening, which is worth fixing.
