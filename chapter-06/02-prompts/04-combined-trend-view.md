# Prompt 4: the combined trend view

```
Add a combined trend section above the cards with:
- a 90-day growth comparison across all channels, including ours
- topics that two or more competitors are betting on
- topics none of them cover that fit our positioning notes
- one short paragraph on where we sit against the group
```

## Then push on what it says

The combined view is where the board starts making claims. Ask for the evidence behind each one:

```
Show me the ten video titles behind that trend, with their view counts.
```

```
How much search demand exists for [topic the board flagged as uncovered],
and who ranks for it outside my competitor list?
```

Both run against live connector data rather than Claude's general knowledge, so each answer gives you more to judge the direction with.

Two things to keep in mind while reading it:

- A topic none of your tracked channels covers is a gap on your board, not a gap in the market. You tracked three to five channels, not the niche.
- Compare channels at the same size before you compare yourself to anyone. The largest channel's playbook works on an audience you do not have, so two competitors of similar size with different results tell you more than the leader does.
