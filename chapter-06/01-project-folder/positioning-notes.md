Wildgrass Yoga sells a paid 8-week course. The YouTube channel
exists to bring in students. We publish about 6 times a month,
roughly a third long videos and two thirds Shorts. The long ones
are 15 to 25 minute flows built around a body area or a daily
routine. The Shorts are technique explainers: chaturanga, float
to forward fold, warm-ups for backbends and splits.
The course call to action sits at the end of the long videos.
Subscribers keep growing but course sales are flat, and we do not
know whether the problem is the channel or the offer.
